Welcome to Wikonomi

You’re here early.

And that means you have a chance to help build something from the ground floor.

Papua New Guinea is full of businesses, products, prices, knowledge, skills and opportunity.

But much of that information is still scattered — on shop shelves, in markets, in Facebook posts, in people’s phones, and in the knowledge we carry with us every day.

Wikonomi exists to bring that knowledge together.

We’re a small PNG startup with a big mission: to help build a living digital database of Papua New Guinea’s local economy — built not just by companies or institutions, but by ordinary people sharing what they see and what they know.

Post a local price

A price posted today could help someone find a better deal tomorrow.

Add a business

A business added to Wikonomi could help a new customer discover it.

Share what you know

A guide from your own experience could save another Papua New Guinean hours of confusion.

Small actions can travel further than we imagine.

And right now, you are joining while the map is still being drawn.

Wikonomi is still young. We’re still building, learning and improving. That means you’re not just another user arriving after everything has already been built.

You’re part of the ground floor.

You can help shape what this becomes.

Snap a price. Add a business. Share what you know. Write a guide. Tell someone about Wikonomi.

One contribution might seem small.

But thousands of people contributing one small piece of knowledge at a time can create something PNG has never had before:

A living map of our local economy, built by Papua New Guineans for Papua New Guinea.

Maybe one day Wikonomi will be something millions of people rely on.

If that day comes, you’ll be able to say:

“I was there when it was just beginning.
And I helped build it.”

Thank you for joining us.
